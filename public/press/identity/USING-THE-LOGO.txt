ANWAR CREATIVE STUDIO: USING THE LOGO

The parts
  The lockup      The hanging a with "Anwar." Use it wherever the studio is named.
  The hanging a   A lowercase a hanging from a headline, with a gold point.
                  For square spaces only: avatars, favicons, app icons.
  The gold point  The full stop of "Anwar." and the point inside the a.

Files
  anwar-lockup-ink.png     dark ink, for light backgrounds
  anwar-lockup-light.png   light ink, for dark backgrounds
  anwar-symbol-ink.svg     the hanging a, dark ink
  anwar-symbol-light.svg   the hanging a, light ink

Please
  Leave clear space around the logo of at least half its height.
  Keep it at least 80 px wide on screen (20 mm in print).
  Use the files as they are: no recoloring, stretching, outlines,
  shadows or effects, and never set the name in another typeface.
  Write the studio's name in text as "Anwar Creative Studio".
