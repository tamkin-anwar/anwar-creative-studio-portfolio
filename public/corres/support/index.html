Corres · Support

How can we help?

Write to support@corres.app and a person reads it, usually within a day. The answers to the common questions are below.

Accounts

Which email accounts work with Corres?

Gmail and Google Workspace, and Outlook.com, Hotmail, Live and Microsoft 365. Add one in Settings → Add Account. iCloud Mail and other providers are coming.

Google says the app isn't verified, or won't let me sign in

While Corres is in beta, Google limits Gmail sign-in to listed testers until its verification is finished. Email us the Gmail address you'd like to use and we'll add it.

My work Outlook account needs an administrator's approval

Some companies only allow apps their IT team has approved. Ask your administrator to approve Corres for your organization, or use a personal Outlook account in the meantime.

How do I remove an account?

Settings → tap the account → Remove Account. Its mail is deleted from your iPhone, and its push notifications are turned off. To revoke access entirely, also remove Corres in your Google or Microsoft account's security settings.

Mail and notifications

I'm not getting notifications

Check Settings → Notifications in Corres (each account has its own setting) and Corres in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications. Outlook mail notifies the moment it arrives. Gmail notifies when iOS lets Corres check in, which is usually within minutes.

Why is an email in Needs You, or not?

Every email in Needs You says why it's there. Move one to fix it: Corres learns from it, and Settings → Sorting → What Corres Has Learned shows each lesson and lets you undo it.

What is the Screener?

First emails from new senders can wait for your yes or no. Turn it on in Settings → Sorting → Screen New Senders, and review them in Review New Senders. Blocking only hides them in Corres; it never changes your email account.

Corres Pro

What's free and what's in Pro?

Mail is free: read, write, search and organize every account. Pro adds the Brief, Needs You, Waiting, Ask, summaries and suggested replies.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Corres. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewing. Apple handles billing and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Open Settings in Corres and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Privacy

Does Corres read my email on a server?

No. Mail goes straight from Google or Microsoft to your iPhone and stays there. Sorting, summaries and Ask run on your iPhone. The full details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Remove your accounts in Settings and delete the app. Nothing of your mail is kept anywhere else. Questions about privacy: privacy@corres.app.
