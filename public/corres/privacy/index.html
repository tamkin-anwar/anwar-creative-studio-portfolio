Corres · Privacy Policy

Your mail stays yours.

Corres reads and sorts your email on your iPhone. It doesn't sell your data, show ads, or send your mail to an AI company.

Effective September 29, 2026. Corres is made by Anwar Creative Studio (“we”).

What Corres accesses

When you connect a Gmail account, you sign in with Google directly; Corres never sees your password. Google gives Corres permission to read, send and organize (archive, label, mark read, move to Trash) your Gmail, which is what an email app needs to work. You can revoke it anytime in your Google Account settings or by removing the account in Corres.

Where your mail is processed

  • On your device. Your messages are downloaded from Google to your iPhone or iPad and stored there, protected by iOS. Sorting, summaries, suggested replies, rewrites, dictation and Ask your mail all run on your device using Apple Intelligence and Apple's on-device speech recognition.
  • Never on our servers, never with AI companies. The contents of your email are not sent to Anwar Creative Studio or to any AI provider.
  • Your sign-in permission is kept in your device's Keychain.

What our servers see

  • New-mail notifications (optional). If you turn on notifications, a small relay stores your device's notification token and the email addresses of your connected accounts, so it can tell your device that something changed. Google tells the relay only that a mailbox changed; it never receives a subject, sender or message. Turning notifications off or removing an account deletes that record.
  • Scheduled sending (when available). If you schedule an email to send later, that one message is held encrypted on our server with a send-only Google permission until its send time, then deleted. It can't be used to read your mailbox.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing of your data.
  • No read receipts: Corres never tells anyone you opened their email. Remote images and tracking pixels in email are blocked until you choose to show them.
  • Your Gmail data is never used to train AI models, ours or anyone else's.

Google user data

Corres's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Purchases

Corres Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we receive no card details. Your plan is verified on your device from Apple's signed receipts.

Deleting your data

Remove an account in Corres (Settings → the account → Remove Account) to delete its mail and settings from your device and its notification record from the relay. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. Nothing is deleted from Gmail itself.

Children

Corres is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page if anything changes and note the date above. Questions or requests about your data: privacy@corres.app.
