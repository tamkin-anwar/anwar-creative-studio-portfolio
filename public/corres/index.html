How Corres reads your mail

The few emails that matter, first.

Most email asks nothing of you. Corres finds the ones that do, says why, and lets the rest step back, so a full inbox reads like a short note.

Questions

Which email accounts work with Corres?

Gmail and Google Workspace, Outlook.com, Hotmail and Live, work or school Microsoft 365, and iCloud Mail. Add as many as you like; they share one inbox. iCloud asks for an app-specific password instead of your Apple Account password, and Corres walks you through making one in about a minute.

What is Postwright?

Postwright is the mail engine we built for Corres. It talks to Gmail, Outlook and iCloud Mail each in its own way, on your iPhone, so your actions are instant, Undo always works, and every account stays in step with the web. It’s why Corres doesn’t need a server of its own to hold your mail.

Does Corres read my email on its servers?

No. Your mail is downloaded from Google, Microsoft or Apple directly to your iPhone and processed there. An optional notification relay only learns that your mailbox changed, never what’s in it. The privacy policy has the details.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. Sorting, Needs You and Waiting work on every supported iPhone. Summaries and drafted replies use Apple Intelligence where your iPhone has it.

Is there an iPad version?

Yes, Corres runs on iPhone and iPad, with a three-column layout on iPad.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Your mail is on your iPhone, so it reads offline, and anything you archive, delete, flag or mark read goes through as soon as you’re back online.

Is there a Mac app?

It’s in the works: a native Mac app, built for the Mac rather than carried over from iPhone, on the same engine and with what you’ve set in Corres following you there through iCloud.

How do I get it?

Corres is in beta on TestFlight. Ask for early access, and it’s coming to the App Store.
