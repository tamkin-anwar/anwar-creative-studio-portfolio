What we hold to

Four promises you can check.

No ads, no trackers, and nothing sold. We’d rather charge for what we make than make money from what you do in it.

Private where it counts.

What moves, and why
The product is the demoCorres for Mac is rebuilt in HTML, the way Linear builds its homepage: eight emails arrive, the five that need nothing fold away, the three that need you rise with their reasons, and Maya’s email opens with a summary and suggested replies. It plays once when you reach it, takes about two seconds, and never loops.
Artha answers backThe pace line draws, then the number settles on $64. Press “Log a $32 lunch” and it rolls to $32, the way the app does. Every figure is real text, readable by screen readers.
Native, not heavyNo video, canvas, WebGL or animation library. Device frames ease in with the new CSS scroll timeline in Chrome and Safari; other browsers show them still. The whole page weighs less than one product video.
Follows your themeBoth app recreations switch with light and dark, so there’s no dark screenshot sitting on a light page. Theme changes are instant, as interface designers recommend.
Calm everywhere elseOnly the products move. No fade-ups on every section, no parallax, no smooth-scroll, no cursor effects; the headline rises once on load.
Respects Reduce MotionWith Reduce Motion on, both demos simply show their finished state.
