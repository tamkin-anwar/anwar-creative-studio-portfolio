What we hold to

Four promises you can check.

Your data isn’t the product.

No ads, no trackers, and nothing sold. We’d rather charge for what we make than make money from what you do in it.

Private where it counts.

Corres sorts, summarizes and drafts on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence, so your mail is never sent to us or to an AI company.

Nothing fails silently.

If something doesn’t go through, you’ll know. An email sent with no signal waits and goes by itself once you’re back online.

Native, and finished.

Written in SwiftUI for iPhone, iPad and Mac, following your text size and working with VoiceOver.

What changed from the live site, and why
The product is the heroCorres on Mac and iPhone and Artha on iPhone replace the particle network, which reads as stock “AI and data” art.
Left-aligned, one buttonThe headline leads at full size; one primary action, everything else a text link, like Apple, Linear and Stripe.
Warm paper by defaultLight like Things, Bear and Craft, with the current near-black when your device is in dark mode. Try the switch at the top.
Tighter typeFraunces set lighter and tighter with its optical-size and soft axes; Inter at medium for actions. Mono only for dates.
Fine print, not chipsOne quiet line under each app instead of a row of pills, the way Nova shows its specs.
Fewer decorationsNo eyebrow labels in capitals, no side dot navigation, amber kept for links only.
