The identity, and where each part goes
AnwarThe lockup

Identifies the studio: header, footer, documents, press. The name hangs from the same line as the a.

The hanging a

Small square places only: the favicon, app and social avatars. Everywhere else it appears inside the lockup.

aa.

The end mark, and only that. Like the square that closes every article in The Economist, it closes the studio’s own writing: the note above, each journal post, a letter. Never in a header, footer or avatar, and never near the hanging a.

Corres from Anwarfrom Anwar.

On corres.app and arthaapp.com: the product leads, the studio signs quietly, with no symbol beside the app’s own icon.

What moves, and why
Real Apple hardwareThe iPhone 18 Pro and the 14-inch MacBook Pro are Apple’s own Product Bezels, used unmodified as Apple’s marketing guidelines require. The app screens behind the glass are live HTML, so they follow light and dark and move.
The product is the demoOn the Mac, eight emails arrive and Corres sorts them into Needs You with the reasons, then opens Maya’s email with a summary and replies. On the iPhone, a new email that needs you arrives as a notification and joins Due soon.
Artha answers backThe pace line draws, the number settles on $64, and “Log a $32 lunch” rolls it to $32, as the app does.
Calm everywhere elseThe devices never move, by Apple’s rule and by taste. Only what’s on their screens does, once, with Reduce Motion respected.
The new identityThe hanging a and Anwar. are one lockup: the symbol is exactly the height of the capitals, its bar on the cap line and its foot on the baseline, so the name hangs from the same line as the a. The gap is two stem widths, and the gold point inside the a is the same size as the gold full stop that ends the name. The point opens the logo; the full stop closes it.
Where each part goesThe lockup in the header and footer; the hanging a alone as the favicon and avatars; a. only as the end mark that closes the studio’s own writing (the closing note, journal posts, letters), never near the hanging a; the gold point at most once in a page’s content, as the full stop of “Built it.”; “Anwar Creative Studio LLC” only as plain text, for legal lines. On the product sites, a quiet “from Anwar.” with no symbol, so it never competes with the Corres or Artha icon.
