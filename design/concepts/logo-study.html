A mark that means something.

The sparkle beside the name today is the same four-point star Google, Microsoft, Slack and ChatGPT use for AI features, so it says “AI” before it says Anwar. These four directions start instead from things only this studio has.

Anwar, from the Arabic root for light: brighter, more luminous.And both apps already carry a point of light: the single point inside the Corres icon, the sun rising in Artha’s.
A · recommended symbol

The hanging a

A Latin a that hangs from a headline, the way every Bengali letter hangs from its matra. Most people read a clean lowercase a; anyone who reads Bengali sees the script’s structure. A point of light sits in its counter.

It belongs to the founder’s own heritage and no one else’s, and it holds up as a crisp a at 16 pixels.

Risks. Without a hand-drawn final pass it could look like imitation script, so a Bengali reader should check it. The bar over the letter could be misread as “ā”.

B · recommended wordmark

Anwar.

The name, set in a tuned Fraunces with the motto’s full stop turned into a gold point of light. “Looked for it. Didn’t love it. Built it.” already ends in full stops; this makes the last one the brand.

Short form for small places: a.

Risks. A name with a colored dot is a familiar device. It becomes ownable only through real custom drawing of the letters (the A apex, the w joins, the r terminal) and consistent use.

C · alternative

Light within

The curved roof line of Bengal’s do-chala buildings, shared across temples, mosques and homes, over two posts, with the point of light in the doorway. A light inside, which is what the name means.

Risks. At favicon size it reads as a house or a temple, and could pass for a real-estate logo. It needs a careful custom drawing to feel like software.

D · alternative

Open A

An A without its crossbar, with the point of light where the bar would be. Simple, strong at every size, and it says Anwar and light in one shape.

Risks. A as a triangle is one of the most common startup marks; the point is what saves it, and only just. The least ownable of the four.

Recommendation

Use A and B together.

The hanging a becomes the symbol: favicon, social avatar, the corner of the press kit, the small “from Anwar” on Corres and Artha. The Anwar. wordmark leads everywhere there’s room. The gold point is the thread through all of it, the same point of light that sits inside both app icons.

  • One color always works; gold is an accent, never needed to read the mark.
  • It stays a quiet stamp next to Corres and Artha instead of competing with them.
  • Before it ships, the letterforms should get a final hand-drawn pass, and a Bengali reader should check that the hanging a reads as respectful, not imitation script.
